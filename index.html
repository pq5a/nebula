Nebula

Discord in its own window, dressed the way you like.

Nebula is a small Windows app that runs Discord in a dedicated window with a custom title bar, three full-client themes and Vencord built in and kept up to date for you.

Theme: Nebula

Three themes that change everything

Nebula, Midnight and Ember recolor the whole client. Text stays readable on every surface, and your pick is remembered for the next launch.

A window that fits the look

Nebula draws its own slim title bar with minimize, maximize and close. Drag it to move the window, or double-click it to maximize.

No system chrome, no clutter

Vencord, always current

Vencord is built in. Each launch Nebula checks for the latest build, verifies it against GitHub's published checksum, and keeps a copy that starts offline.

Verified downloads

Links go where they belong

Discord stays inside Nebula. Any other website opens as a new tab in Firefox, so your browsing never gets lost in the app.
