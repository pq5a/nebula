Discord in its own window, dressed the way you like.

Nebula is a small Windows app that runs Discord in a dedicated window with a custom title bar, eight full-client themes, a low-power mode and Vencord built in and kept up to date for you.

Your whole client, in your palette

Pick a theme from the settings menu in the top-left corner and the entire Discord window follows: backgrounds, text and accents.

Eight themes that change everything

Nebula, Midnight, Ember, Aurora, Rose, Forest, Solar and Graphite recolor the whole client. Text stays readable on every surface, and your pick is remembered for the next launch.

Settings in the corner

A settings icon sits at the top-left of the title bar. One click opens a small menu with the theme list and the low-power switch. Nothing is hidden behind a separate window.

Low-power mode

When Nebula is tabbed out, Discord's animations drop to 10 frames a second, which saves CPU and GPU time in the background. It is on by default, and you can turn it off in the settings menu.

A window that fits the look

Nebula draws its own slim title bar with minimize, maximize and close. Drag it to move the window, or double-click it to maximize.

Saved accounts

Keep several Discord accounts in the account dropdown and switch between them in one click. Passwords are encrypted with Windows DPAPI, so only your Windows user can read them.

Vencord, always current

Vencord is built in. Each launch Nebula checks for the latest build, verifies it against GitHub's published checksum, and keeps a copy that starts offline.

Links go where they belong

Discord stays inside Nebula. Any other website opens as a new tab in Firefox, so your browsing never gets lost in the app.

Up and running in three steps

  1. DownloadClick the button at the top to save Nebula.exe.
  2. Run itOpen Nebula.exe. If SmartScreen appears, choose "Run anyway". Later versions offer to update themselves on startup.
  3. Make it yoursClick the settings icon at the top-left of the window and pick a theme.
